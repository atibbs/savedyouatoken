## Why

The documented user journey supports a recurring product but does not prove retention or willingness
to pay. A structured pilot with three to five production teams must validate the operational loop
before the project activates billing or builds a durable hosted control plane.

## What Changes

- Define participant qualification, informed data boundaries, onboarding, support, and exit
  procedures for a small production pilot.
- Deliver workflow labels, prompt-free retained reports, release comparisons, one regression
  notification, a weekly summary, and CI-policy handoff using lightweight or manual operations.
- Instrument the pilot funnel from installation through mature measurement, action, comparison,
  retained use, and willingness-to-pay evidence.
- Establish explicit success, failure, and stop criteria for proceeding to Developer Monitor.
- Record findings without weakening prompt privacy or silently expanding data collection.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `monitor-validation-pilot`: A repeatable, privacy-bounded pilot workflow and evidence standard for
  validating recurring Monitor value.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Primarily pilot documentation, consent/data-handling procedures, prompt-free reporting tools, and
product analytics definitions. The free-tier static/zero-cost invariant is unchanged. The pilot may
use manually operated or temporary services, but must not create an unreviewed always-on production
dependency or activate paid billing.
