# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in savedyouatoken welcoming and free from harassment for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus disagreement on ideas and evidence.
- Protect other people's privacy and confidential information.
- Do not use sexualized language, personal attacks, intimidation, sustained disruption, harassment,
  or unwanted public or private contact.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project, including
issues, pull requests, discussions, release channels, and community events.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the repository maintainer through a private GitHub
Security Advisory at
<https://github.com/atibbs/savedyouatoken/security/advisories/new>. Do not open a public issue when a
report contains personal or sensitive information.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, protect the reporter's privacy as far as practical,
and may edit or remove contributions, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation, or ban
participants. A maintainer named in a report will not adjudicate that report; an independent
reviewer will be requested before action is taken.
